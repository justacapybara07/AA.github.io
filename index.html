A letter I should have written sooner

For the Person
Who Means More

Aarya, this is for you.
01 · Before anything else

There’s something I need to say.

I’ve been thinking about the way I’ve acted, and I don’t want to hide behind excuses or pretend I got everything right. You deserve a sincere apology from me.

02 · I’m sorry

I’m sorry for being pushy.

I know I’ve texted you too much and kept reaching out when I should have given you more room. Even if my intentions came from a good place, I understand that my messages may have felt overwhelming. I’m sorry for putting that pressure on you.

I should have respected your space better.
03 · The truth behind it

Your friendship means a lot to me.

I haven’t had many friendships that felt as meaningful to me as yours does. I think I got scared of losing something important, and instead of handling that feeling maturely, I kept texting. That explains what happened, but it doesn’t make it fair to you.

04 · Taking responsibility

You didn’t owe me constant replies.

You have your own life, your own time, and your own feelings. I should never make you feel guilty for being busy, needing space, or not knowing what to say. I’ll work on giving you that space instead of expecting reassurance from you.

I want to do better, not just say I will.
05 · What I kept inside

You matter to me, Aarya.

Maybe I haven’t always known how to show that in the right way. I’m grateful that you became such an important friend to me, and I’m sorry that my fear of losing your friendship may have made things harder instead of better.

06 · No expectations

I’m not asking you to feel a certain way.

I don’t expect anything from you; I just wanted you to know what’s in my heart. You don’t have to answer right away, forgive me immediately, or say anything just to make me feel better. Your honesty and comfort matter, too.

07 · If you can forgive me

I hope, in time, we can be okay.

I hope you can forgive me, but I understand that forgiveness is yours to give, in your own time. Whatever you need from me now—especially space—I’ll respect it. I’m truly sorry, Aarya.

Thank you for being a friend who means so much to me.
♡
For you, Aarya

I don’t ask you to feel the same…

I just hope you understand my heart.

I’m sorry. I care about you. And I’ll do my best to show that with respect, not pressure.

♡

Whatever happens next,

I’ll still wish you happiness.

No pressure. No expectation. Just an honest apology.
